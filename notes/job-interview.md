# Job Interview

## Questions from me

About position - Technical:

- What is your tech stack?
- What OS do you use? Can I use Linux/Mac?
- Do you do testing / QA?
- Documentation?

About position - non-Technical:

- Development methodology (Scrum, kanban, waterfall etc)
- Development cycle? (Design review > develop > test > QA > production)
- How much and how do you use AI?
- Are there team members in other locations?
- Who are stakeholders you work with?
- Any emergencies - shifts?

Working conditions - Personal:

- What is a usual day for you? (Daily work pace)
- What is an ideal/perfect workday like for you?
- How fast paced you’d say? (scale from 1-5)
- Hardest and easiest things you find working at your company?
- Previous experience with international teams?

Working conditions - HR:

- Remote work? / Flexibility?
- Flexible hours?
- How can I get promoted?
- Chance of changing teams?
- Other office visits?
- KPIs?

Hiring process:

- Salary?
- How long does it take average?
- Do you have other candidates?
- Leave / join ratio

## Possible questions to me

- Why do you want to work at new company?
- Why do you want to change your current job now?
- Why did you leave company-X?
