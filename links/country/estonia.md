## My recommendations to my guests

See:

- Old town tour
- Estonian Open Air museum (https://evm.ee/en), sea-side forest with old cultural village houses

Do:

- Scooter riding
- Minigolf (ie in Nautica - <https://parkminigolf.ee>)
- VR (ie <https://mustamae.another-world.com>)
- Shooting range, for example: 
    - <https://laskmine.ee/en/> (been there before)
    - <https://gungarage.ee/en/packages>
    - <https://tlk.ee/en/product-categories/shooting-packages/>
